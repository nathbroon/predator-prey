Preset

Rules, as in the Haskell

  • Each forager and predator looks at its own cell and its 8 Moore neighbours on a wrap-around lattice. A cell's appeal is its blank gene if empty, otherwise the lowest gene among the species there. It moves to the most attractive cell, breaking ties at random. The header comment describes a roulette wheel over the neighbourhood instead; the Movement menu switches between the two.
  • Predators sharing a cell with foragers eat them all; a random predator gets each kill. Foragers on food (with no predator) eat it. Food is passive.
  • Every step costs energy; an organism already at zero energy is removed. Anything at or above the mating energy breeds: it pays the birth cost, and an equal number of offspring are drawn by energy-weighted roulette.
  • Offspring are born on the parent's cell. With probability pc a pair blend their genomes at a random point between them; with probability pm a genome is pulled halfway toward a pure attraction vertex or the centre.
